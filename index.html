Business Loan & Working Capital

Unsecured business loans, MSME credit, and CC/OD limits.

MSME / Working Capital
🪪 KYC & Promoter Proofs
  • 📄
    PAN Card Mandatory for firm, proprietor, and all active partners / directors
  • 📄
    Aadhaar Card UIDAI Aadhaar of all promoters with active mobile link for e-sign
  • 📄
    Passport Photos & Residence Proof Recent color photographs & electricity bill / rent agreement
📑 Business Registration & Legal
  • 📄
    GST Certificate & Returns GSTIN registration certificate and active 12-month GSTR-3B filings
  • 📄
    Udyam Aadhaar & Shop Act MSME certificate & Gumastadhara / municipal trade clearance
  • 📄
    Entity Constitution Partnership deed / MOA & AOA with Certificate of Incorporation
📊 Financials & Banking Statements
  • 📄
    Last 3 Years Audited Financials Balance Sheet, P&L, Audit Reports & CA 3CA/3CB forms
  • 📄
    Last 3 Years Income Tax Returns Complete ITR acknowledgements with computation of income
  • 📄
    12 Months Bank Statements Statements for all Current & Savings operative bank accounts
💡 DK Advisory Tip: Keep your 12 months GST-3B returns reconciled with bank turnover for fastest approval.

Home Loan & Construction

New flat purchase, bungalow construction, and plot loans.

Residential Finance
🪪 Applicant KYC & Identity
  • 📄
    PAN Card & Aadhaar Card Mandatory for primary applicant and all co-applicants / co-owners
  • 📄
    Passport Photographs Recent colored passport photos of all applicants
💵 Income & Banking Proofs
  • 📄
    Salaried Income Proof Last 6 months salary slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), and 12-month salary bank statement
  • 📄
    Self-Employed Financials Last 3 years ITR with computation, Balance Sheet, P&L, and 12 months current bank statements
🏗️ Property & Title Documents
  • 📄
    Agreement to Sell / Allotment Letter Registered agreement to sell or builder allotment letter
  • 📄
    Approved Building Plan (RMC / RUDA) Sanctioned municipal blueprint and approved layout
  • 📄
    Title Search & N.A. Order 30-year title clearance report and Non-Agricultural order
💡 DK Advisory Tip: Property title clearance in Rajkot requires checking the 30-year index register and RMC/RUDA development zoning. We handle this verification.

Property Collateral
